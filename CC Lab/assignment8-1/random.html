What I learned in this class

At the start of the semester, a variable was just a strange word to me. Now I think of it as a little box with a name on it, and most of my sketches are about changing what is inside those boxes over time.

What changed for me

Coding used to feel like something I could follow in class but couldn't use on my own. After redoing my assignments step by step, I can now look at my own storyboard and figure out how to build it. I think I just need more practice to get used to this new tool.
